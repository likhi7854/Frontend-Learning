Movie Titles

Mirai

Description: Based on historical lore tied to Emperor Ashoka, the film follows a street-smart young protagonist (Teja Sajja) tasked with preventing dark forces from obtaining secret sacred texts that grant absolute power.

Cast:

Starring:

  • Teja Sajja
  • Manchu Manoj
  • Ritika Nayak

Release Date: September 12, 2025

Genre: Mytho-Superhero / Fantasy

Rating: PG (Parental Guidance suggested due to thematic elements and mild scary images)

List of Main Features:

  • Time-Traveling Family Dynamics: Uses magic realism to bridge generations, connecting Kun with his family's ancestors and future descendants.
  • Child-Centric Perspective: Masterfully frames emotional jealousy, tantrums, and growth through the imaginative lens of a preschooler.
  • Academy Award-Nominated Visuals: Produced by Studio Chizu, featuring lush, vibrant animation blending 2D art with detailed backgrounds.
  • Relatable Family Struggles: Tackles modern parenting challenges, shifting household roles, and career balances.

Review Paragraph:

Mirai delivers a visually slick, ambitious Indian superhero spectacle that blends ancient legends with modern cinematic flair. Lead actor Teja Sajja anchors the narrative with high charisma, carrying the action heavy-lifting smoothly alongside a powerful performance by Manchu Manoj as the antagonist. While the narrative occasionally leans into familiar genre beats and relies heavily on high-energy set pieces, the film succeeds as a thrilling, visual-effects-driven experience backed by a pulse-pounding background score.

Kuberaa

Description: A socio-political crime thriller that contrasts the ultra-wealthy elite with the urban underprivileged. The story revolves around Deva, an honest beggar who becomes an unwitting pawn in a power struggle involving a ruthless business tycoon, corrupt politicians, and systemic greed.

Cast:

Starring:

  • Dhanush
  • Nagarjuna
  • Rashmika Mandanna

Release Date: June 20, 2025

Genre: Drama / Crime Thriller

Rating: UA13+ / PG-13 equivalent

List of Main Features:

  • Star-Studded Cast: Stars Dhanush, Nagarjuna Akkineni, Rashmika Mandanna, and Jim Sarbh.
  • Departure for Sekhar Kammula: Known for lighthearted dramas, director Sekhar Kammula takes a gritty, darker turn exploring class divide and systemic corruption.
  • Atmospheric Score & Cinematography: Features an intense background score by Devi Sri Prasad and wide, immersive framing by Niketh Bommireddy.
  • Multi-Lingual Production: Shot simultaneously in Telugu and Tamil.

Review:

Kuberaa is a compelling and ambitious social thriller that fearlessly tackles topics of wealth inequality, corporate greed, and systemic exploitation. Dhanush delivers a standout, raw performance as an invisible member of society caught in a complex web of power, grounded effectively alongside a restrained performance by Nagarjuna and a menacing turn by Jim Sarbh. While its long runtime and a slightly rushed climax keep it from absolute perfection, its sharp commentary, gritty atmosphere, and strong direction make it a memorable cinematic entry.

They Call Him OG

Description: Set in Mumbai's brutal underworld, the film follows Ojas Gambheera ("OG"), a legendary and ruthless gangster who resurfaces after disappearing for ten years to reclaim his turf and eliminate a rising syndicate threatening the city.

Cast:

Starring:

  • Pawan Kalyan
  • Emraan Hashmi
  • Priyanka Mohan

Release Date: September 25, 2025

Genre: Action / Underworld Thriller

Rating:UA16+ / PG-13 equivalent (features intense violence, stylized gunfights, and dark underworld themes)

List of Main Features:

  • Pawan Kalyan's Return to Action: Features Pawan Kalyan in a gritty, intense antihero persona reminiscent of his classic action roots.
  • High-Octane Visual Style: Directed by Sujeeth (Saaho), focusing on slick, international-style action set pieces and high-contrast cinematography.
  • Star Ensemble: Co-stars Emraan Hashmi as the main antagonist, alongside Priyanka Arul Mohan, Prakash Raj, and Arjun Das.
  • Pumping Musical Score: Driven by a fast-paced, atmospheric soundtrack composed by Thaman S.

Review:

They Call Him OG is a high-octane action film that delivers on its promise of intense sequences and compelling characters. Pawan Kalyan shines as the titular character, bringing depth and complexity to a role that could have been one-dimensional. The film's portrayal of Mumbai's underworld is gritty and authentic, with Emraan Hashmi providing a strong supporting performance. While the plot may be predictable at times, the film's energy and visual spectacle make it an entertaining watch.
